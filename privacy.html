Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Version 1 · Effective 21 November 2026 · Applies to shoof.pages.dev and the installed Shoof app

The short version

  • Shoof is free. No ads, no paid plans, and we never sell or rent your data.
  • Your designs stay on your device. They are saved in your browser, not on our servers.
  • You only need an account for the AI tools that run on our servers (Describe it, the Presentation maker and Ask Shoof chat). Editing, templates, downloads and the on-device AI tools work without one.
  • For an account we keep your email address, your name and your Google account number, plus a daily count of your AI uses. That's all.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the editor (⋯ menu → Account → Delete my account). It is removed at once.

By creating an account you agree to this Privacy Policy and to the Terms of Use below.

1. Who runs Shoof

Shoof is a free design editor made by an independent developer and its contributors. It is not a company and does not make money from users. Where this page says "we" or "us", it means the people who run Shoof. The way to reach us is in section 13.

2. What stays on your device

These are saved only in your own browser's storage. We cannot see them:

  • your designs, pages, projects and brand kits
  • pictures you upload, and pictures made by the tools that run on your device (background remover, upscaler, AI fill, lifting objects, text reader)
  • your settings, recent fonts and colours, and what Shoof learns about your own style

If you clear your browser data, or use a private window, these are gone. Download or save your designs (File → Save) if you want a copy. A "Copy a link" share packs the design into the link itself; it is not uploaded to us, but anyone you give the link to can open it.

3. Accounts

Why we ask you to sign in

The AI tools in section 4 run on our servers and cost money to run, even when you pay nothing. An account lets us give everyone a fair daily allowance and keep bots and abuse away, so Shoof can stay free.

How you sign in

You sign in with your Google account. Google tells us your email address, your name and an account number that Google uses for you. We never see your Google password, and we do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else.

Deleting your account

In the editor open the ⋯ menu → Account → Delete my account. Your account and its usage counts are deleted from our database at once. Your designs on your device are not touched. Any copies in Cloudflare's normal backups are overwritten within their usual cycle (at most 30 days).

4. What the AI tools send

Only when you use them, these tools send the following to our server, where a free AI model on Cloudflare (Workers AI) writes an answer:

  • Describe it: the words you typed and the names of a few matching templates.
  • Presentation maker: your topic, or the text you pasted.
  • Ask Shoof chat: your last few chat messages and a short text summary of your design (its words, colours, fonts and layout). For questions about how the design looks, also a small picture of the design. Your uploaded photos are never sent on their own.

These requests are not stored, with one exception: when a chat answer changes your design, a cleaned copy of your request (links, emails and long numbers removed) and the changes made are kept without any account or IP address. Thumbs up, thumbs down and Undo on those answers help Shoof give better answers to everyone. Unrated ones are removed after about three weeks.

Don't put private or sensitive information (ID numbers, passwords, health details) into AI requests. AI answers can be wrong, so check them before you publish.

5. Other things on our servers

  • Feedback box: your message, its type (idea, problem, question), which page it came from, your browser's language and name, and an email address only if you choose to give one. We use this to fix and improve Shoof and to answer you.
  • Learning statistics: counts of which font and colour swaps people keep, as short codes. No text, pictures, account or IP address.
  • Fair-use counters: to stop floods of requests, Cloudflare's short-term cache counts requests per network address per day. These counters expire within 24 hours.

7. Cookies and storage

  • sh_sess: keeps you signed in for 30 days. Only set when you sign in.
  • sh_oauth: protects the sign-in step for up to 10 minutes.
  • sh_pass: remembers for 30 minutes that you passed the bot check, so chat isn't interrupted.
  • Your browser's local storage, for everything in section 2.

We use no advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, so there's no cookie banner.

8. What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade your data.
  • Show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • Send you marketing emails.
  • Upload your designs or photos to our servers, except as described in section 4.
  • Use your designs to train AI models.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct it (signing in again updates your name and email from Google)
  • delete it (you can do this yourself; see section 3)
  • stop using it, by deleting your account or simply not using the AI tools

We rely on your agreement (given when you create an account) and on our interest in keeping a free service fair and safe. You can withdraw your agreement at any time by deleting your account. Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law), you can also complain to your data protection authority. We'd be glad if you contacted us first so we can put things right.

10. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). The sign-in cookie can't be read by scripts and is signed so it can't be forged. The database is reachable only from Shoof's own server code. Sign-in secrets are kept on Cloudflare, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure. If we ever find a breach that affects your data, we will tell affected users and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Age

You must be at least 13 to create an account, or older if the law where you live says so (for example 16 in some EU countries). Shoof does not knowingly keep data about younger children; if you think a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.
