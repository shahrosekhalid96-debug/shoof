Shoof is a design editor that runs in your browser. Make posts, posters, logos and presentations from 122 templates, then download them in the format you need.

Start designing See examples

Nothing to install. Your designs stay on your device.

What's inside

The tools other apps charge for, free.

Everything below is included. There is no paid plan and no locked button.

122 templates

Posts, stories, posters, menus, certificates and pitch decks. Every part of every template can be changed.

Logo maker

Type your name and choose from 24 logo ideas at a time. Edit the one you like or save it to your brand.

Brand kit

Save your colours, fonts and logo once. Apply them to any design in one click.

Bulk create

Paste a table from Excel and get one design per row: name cards, price tags, certificates.

Background remover

Cut the background out of a photo. It runs on your own device, so the photo is never uploaded.

Download any way

PNG, JPG, PDF, SVG and editable PowerPoint. Resize one design for four social sizes at once.

How it works

Three steps, no sign-up.

Open the editor and you are already working.

  1. Pick a starting point

    Choose a template or a blank page in the size you need.

  2. Make it yours

    Change the words, colours, photos and fonts. Add icons, mockups and charts.

  3. Download it

    Save the picture or file, or keep the design to edit again later.

Ready when you are.

It opens in a second and costs nothing. Arabic and English are both supported.
